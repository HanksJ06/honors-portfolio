Year in review 2026

Over the past year, I have grown significantly as a developing Information Technology professional through my academic coursework, technical projects, internship experience, and career exploration. One of the most impactful experiences was applying my technical knowledge to real-world situations. Outside of my academic and professional experiences, I have continued exploring game development, networking, and cybersecurity through personal projects and independent learning. These activities have helped me build technical expertise while strengthening my ability to learn new technologies and adapt to unfamiliar challenges.

In my coursework at the University of Cincinnati, I continued to develop my critical thinking and technical skills through networking, systems administration, and cybersecurity-related projects. Completing labs that involved configuring servers, troubleshooting network issues, and working with Linux environments helped me become more confident in diagnosing problems and finding effective solutions. These experiences reinforced the importance of attention to detail and professionalism when working with technology.

Outside of the classroom, I explored personal interests in game development and cybersecurity. Designing game concepts and planning software projects allowed me to strengthen my creativity, project planning, and self-directed learning skills. At the same time, my growing interest in cybersecurity encouraged me to learn more about securing systems and networks, helping me connect concepts from my networking studies to real-world security challenges.

One of my greatest strengths this year has been my willingness to learn new technologies and take on unfamiliar challenges. I have also improved my ability to collaborate with others and communicate technical information to both technical and non-technical audiences. Looking ahead, I would like to further develop my leadership skills, deepen my cybersecurity knowledge, and gain additional hands-on experience through internships, research opportunities, or industry certifications. My goal is to continue building both my technical expertise and professional skills so that I can contribute effectively to future teams and projects within the IT field.
